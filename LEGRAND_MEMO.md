# Legrand valuation memo

**29 May 2026 · Euronext Paris · €147.65 ex-dividend close**

**View.** The dated DCF values Legrand at **€92.42 per share**. The **€146.46 bull case** approaches the close and assumes stronger growth, margin expansion and a lower cost of capital together. At the observed price, the bull case leaves little room for disappointment.

**Scenario assumptions.** FY2026 sales growth is 8% / 11% / 13% in bear / base / bull. The base combines 6% organic, 7% acquired growth and a 2% FX drag, consistent with May guidance. FY2030 EBIT margins are 18.5% / 19.8% / 20.5%, anchored to the 19.1% reported Q1 margin. WACC is 8.75% / 7.70% / 6.75% and terminal growth is 1.5% / 2.0% / 2.5%. The base WACC uses a 3% EUR risk-free rate and assumed beta of 1.10, 5% equity risk premium and 15% debt weight. **Why €83.30 versus €173.28?** The original FY2025-based scenario tapered growth from 6% to 3% and held EBIT margin at 19%. The peer median applies 36.83× trailing earnings to Legrand’s €4.705 diluted EPS and imports the market pricing of Schneider Electric, Eaton and Hubbell. Even the updated DCF would need **4.63% perpetual growth** to match that median at base WACC and unchanged forecast cash flows. Evidence of sustained data-center demand, higher reported margins and attractive cash returns on acquisitions would support higher operating forecasts. Slower orders or weak cash conversion would pull them down.
