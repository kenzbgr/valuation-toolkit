# Snapshot provenance

`financials.csv` uses FY2025 income statement, cash flow and year-end balance sheet data, in millions of local currency, except EPS and margins. Shares are year-end common shares outstanding in millions, excluding treasury shares where reported. EPS is diluted. Dates in `available_by` are conservative availability bounds. The Legrand date is the registration document filing date. The other rows use the day before the valuation as a common cutoff for already released FY2025 statements.

`prices.csv` records 29 May 2026 unadjusted closes with a source URL per row. Euronext Paris quotes are in EUR and the two U.S. peers are in USD. The yfinance comparison uses the exact trading date and the Close column with automatic adjustment disabled. The frozen financials use vendor annual columns only where indicated. Those columns were collected after the valuation date and may reflect later standardization or revisions to FY2025 figures. The benchmark uses an annual base and ignores interim updates. It is a dated price comparison with an annual snapshot.

## Financial sources

For the Stock Analysis rows, the three links point to the annual income statement, balance sheet and cash flow statement. Debt includes leases where provided in total debt. Cash includes short-term investments in standardized rows. Legrand uses the issuer’s reported cash and equivalents. Minority interests use book values. Other nonoperating assets are zero in the bundled model, so separately valued investments, pensions and other claims require further diligence. Treat debt, cash and share count conventions as part of the valuation.

## Forecast conventions

Growth and margin paths are analyst scenarios informed by annual results and business mix. The electrical-equipment forecasts start with relatively strong growth and fade. The automotive cases assume slower growth and a margin recovery. The health care cases retain growth and margins near the annual operating base with some expansion. Utility growth is slower, with larger recurring reinvestment. D&A, capex and working capital rates are normalized forecast inputs. Historical EBIT and EBITDA provide context and peer denominators. Forecast cash flows use the editable margin path.

The common EUR risk-free input of 3.00% is in line with the Bundesbank’s 10-year fitted federal-security yield on 29 May. Beta, equity risk premium, debt costs, tax rates and capital weights are explicit scenario inputs. The base case uses fixed target weights chosen independently of the comparison price. Terminal FCFF grows from year-five FCFF at a constant rate, with no separate long-run return-on-capital model. The annual discount periods ignore the partial first fiscal year and changes in cash, debt and shares after the balance sheet date. [Bundesbank capital market indicators](https://statistiken.bundesbank.de/caas/v1/media/809524/data/cb745586c4d354f193bd7b9790975b5b)
