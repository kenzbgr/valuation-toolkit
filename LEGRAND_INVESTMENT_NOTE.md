# Legrand valuation — investment note

Kenz Bougherra · Independent project · Valuation date: 29 May 2026

**Conclusion.** The base DCF implies **€92.42 per share**, 37.4% below the €147.65 ex-dividend close. Higher sales narrow the gap. Matching the close through margins alone would require a **9.07-percentage-point increase in each forecast year**, given the other base assumptions.

## Growth scenario at €15bn of 2030 sales

Legrand aimed for around €15bn of 2030 sales. The scenario keeps 2026 sales as modeled and assumes 9.27% annual sales growth from 2027 through 2030. It retains the base EBIT margin path, reinvestment ratios, WACC and terminal growth. Before incremental deal costs, growth closes about 20% of the base-case gap to the share price.

The costed case buys half of each year’s incremental sales above the base growth rate applied to the prior year’s scenario sales. At 2.0× sales, that implies €1.87bn of undiscounted acquisition outlays. These are deducted from 2027–2030 cash flows. The model assumes full-year revenue contributions, base margins on acquired sales and no incremental deal spend in terminal cash flow.

## Base DCF and reverse DCF

The five-year unlevered DCF uses FY2025 and Q1 2026 disclosures. Base sales growth fades from 11.0% in 2026 to 4.0% in 2030. Reported EBIT margin rises from 19.2% to 19.8%. WACC is 7.70% and terminal growth is 2.0%. Cash flows after 29 May are discounted on an ACT/365 basis. Terminal value is 79.0% of enterprise value. The original Python bear and bull cases give €55.88 and €146.46 per share.

In the reverse DCF, one additional point of EBIT margin in each forecast year adds €6.09 per share. The margin-only result implies a 28.87% reported EBIT margin in 2030, versus 19.1% in 2025. Growth and margins could contribute jointly.

## Equity bridge and trading comparables

Base enterprise value of €29,556.1m less €5,364.8m of adjusted net debt and €33.2m of minority interests gives €24,158.1m of equity value across 261.395m shares. Adjusted net debt includes the detached dividend and a €300m allowance for announced acquisitions, less retained cash estimated by prorating April–December cash generation.

Schneider Electric, Eaton and Hubbell imply €147.18–€205.77 per share on FY2025 EV/EBITDA and €134.72–€180.37 on FY2025 P/E. The EV/EBITDA comparison uses Legrand’s FY2025 equity bridge. Differences in business mix and geography limit comparability.
